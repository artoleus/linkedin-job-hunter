JobTrail Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

In short: JobTrail keeps everything it stores in your own browser. We do not receive, see or sell your job-search data. If you bought JobTrail, we (or our payment provider) hold your purchase details.

1. Who we are

[BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS] ("we") supplies JobTrail. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

2. Data the extension stores on your computer

JobTrail stores the following only in your browser's local extension storage, on your device:

  • your settings, including your name, email address, phone number, home town and target roles;
  • your saved answers to application questions (for example right to work, experience, salary expectation);
  • records of your job applications, notes and follow-up reminders;
  • records of connection requests and welcome messages, including the names, job-search roles and profile links of the people involved;
  • job opportunities found in your LinkedIn feed.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. JobTrail does not use analytics, tracking or advertising services. The extension only acts on LinkedIn pages you have open, and the information it enters into LinkedIn forms or messages is sent to LinkedIn by your own browser, as part of your use of LinkedIn. LinkedIn's handling of that information is covered by LinkedIn's privacy policy.

Because we never receive this data, we are not the controller of it. You control it: you can export it (CSV and settings backup), and deleting the extension's data or uninstalling the extension removes it from your device.

3. Information about other people

JobTrail stores limited information about people you connect with or message (names, headlines or roles, profile links, and the messages you send). This stays on your device. If you use it in a way that data protection law applies to (for example, for business purposes), you are responsible for complying with that law.

4. Data we do handle

If you buy JobTrail or contact us, we process:

  • Purchase details (name, email address, billing country, what you bought and when) so we can supply your licence, provide updates and meet tax and accounting obligations. Payments are processed by [PAYMENT PROVIDER, e.g. Lemon Squeezy / Paddle], which acts as [reseller / merchant of record] and has its own privacy policy. We never see your full card details.
  • Support messages you send us, so we can help you.
  • Licence checks [only if you add licence-key checking: when the extension verifies your licence key, it sends the key and the extension version to our licence provider. No job-search data is sent.]

Our lawful bases under the UK GDPR are: performing our contract with you (supplying the licence and support), legal obligation (tax and accounting records), and our legitimate interests (preventing licence misuse). We keep purchase records for 6 years for tax purposes and support emails for up to 2 years.

5. Your rights

You have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict our use of the personal data we hold about you, to object to it, and to data portability. To use them, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL]. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

6. Security

Data stored by the extension is protected by your browser and device security. Exported files (backups and CSVs) contain personal details: keep them somewhere safe.

7. Changes

We will update this policy if what we do with data changes, and show the date of the latest version at the top.
